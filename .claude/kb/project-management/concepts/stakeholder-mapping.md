# Stakeholder Mapping

> **Purpose**: Build and maintain the influence/interest matrix that drives the communication plan
> **Confidence**: 0.80
> **MCP Validated**: not applicable — house distillation, not MCP-fed

## Overview

A stakeholder matrix is not a contact list — it is a decision tool. Influence and interest, plotted against each other, determine how much effort goes into engaging each person, and through what channel.

## Quick Reference

| Quadrant | Engagement strategy | Channel |
|----------|----------------------|---------|
| Manage closely (high/high) | Direct involvement in decisions | Meetings, direct message |
| Keep satisfied (high influence, low interest) | Enough to prevent surprise re-engagement | Periodic status update |
| Keep informed (low influence, high interest) | Regular but lightweight updates | Progress reports, digest |
| Monitor (low/low) | Minimal, on-demand only | Availability, not push |

## Common Mistakes

### Wrong

Building the matrix once at kickoff and never touching it again — influence and interest shift as scope, budget, and organizational priorities change.

### Correct

Revisit the matrix at each Monitoring & Controlling checkpoint; a stakeholder who was "keep informed" often becomes "manage closely" once their budget is affected.
